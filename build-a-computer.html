For teachers

Teacher notes

Learning goal

Students name the main parts inside a computer and explain what each part does, using everyday comparisons.

Curriculum link

Australian Curriculum v9 · Digital Technologies · Years 5–6

AC9TDI6K01 Investigate the main internal components of common digital systems and their function.

Also works as an introduction for Years 3–4.

Class discussion questions

  1. Why does a computer need both RAM and storage? What would happen if it only had RAM?
  2. Which computer part is most like a part of your body? Why do you think so?
  3. A laptop has a battery, but a desktop computer has a power supply. Why do you think that is? What about tablets and phones?

How it works

  • Choose Desktop or Laptop above the tray. The laptop has smaller, flatter parts and a battery instead of a power supply. Building both is a good way to compare.
  • Easy shows the part names on the slots. Challenge hides them, so students use the shapes. Challenge also hides names in the quiz.
  • Students can drag parts, or tap a part and then tap a slot. Keyboard works too (Tab, then Enter).
  • The quiz has no timer. Stars count answers right on the first try.
  • Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Sound is off until a student turns it on.
